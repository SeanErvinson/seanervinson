# Privacy Policy for ShuttleWaddle

**Last updated:** 2025-12-31

This Privacy Policy describes how ShuttleWaddle ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

We have built this App for personal use among friends to manage and enjoy badminton sessions. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

## 1. Information We Collect

The App requires specific permissions to function correctly. By using the App, you grant us permission to access specific features on your device.

### A. Audio Recording (Microphone Access)
* **Permission:** `android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO`
* **Why we need it:** This permission is required to enable voice commands to modify scoreboard.
* **Data Handling:** Audio data is processed locally on your device. We do not store your audio recordings on external servers, nor do we use them for advertising purposes.

### B. Device and Technical Information
Since the App is built using **Expo**, it may collect standard technical data required for the app to run smoothly, such as:
* Device model and operating system version.
* Crash logs and performance data (to help us fix bugs).

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected solely to:
* Provide and maintain the functionality of the App.
* Improve the App based on crash reports and feedback.
* [If applicable: Allow you to share scores/audio with other users].

**We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to others.**

## 3. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services that collect information used to identify you. The App uses the following third-party services:

* **Google Play Services:** Used for app distribution and core functionality.
* **Expo:** The framework used to build the app, which may collect metadata about app usage.

Links to privacy policies of third-party service providers used by the app:

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide the App's functionality. If you wish to have any personal data removed, please contact us, and we will delete it immediately.

## 5. Security

We value your trust in providing us your information and strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

## 6. Children’s Privacy

This App does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will immediately delete this from our records.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
